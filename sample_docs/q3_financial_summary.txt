# Apex Financial Partners - Q3 Performance & Investment Summary

## Executive Overview
During the third quarter, Apex Financial delivered record quarterly revenue of $48.2 million, representing a 24.5% year-over-year increase compared to Q3 of the prior fiscal year ($38.7 million). Net profit margins expanded by 320 basis points to reach 18.4%.

## Segment Highlights

### 1. Private Wealth Management
- Assets under management (AUM) reached $1.85 billion, up 14% year-to-date.
- Net new advisory inflows totaled $112 million during Q3.
- Average management fee was maintained at 0.72% annualized.

### 2. Commercial Real Estate Lending
- Originated $145 million in new senior-secured loans across multifamily and light industrial sectors.
- Weighted average loan-to-value (LTV) ratio sits at a conservative 58.4%.
- Delinquency rate over 30 days remains under 0.28%, substantially outperforming peer banking benchmarks.

### 3. Quantitative Alpha Strategy Fund
- Net Q3 return: +7.8% (against S&P 500 benchmark of +4.2%).
- Annualized Sharpe Ratio: 1.94.
- Maximum drawdown experienced during the quarter: -1.6%.

## Capital Allocation & Forward Guidance
- Board approved an increase in quarterly dividend to $0.45 per common share.
- Share buyback authorization remains active with $25 million in remaining dry powder through Q4.
- Full-year revenue target has been revised upward to $185 - $190 million.
